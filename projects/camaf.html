Adaptive traffic control without centralizing the raw video.

Context-Aware Multi-Agent Federated Learning for Adaptive Traffic Signal Control — an end-to-end system combining edge perception, intersection coordination, federated optimization, encryption and SUMO simulation.

YOLOv11nBoT-SORTEclipse SUMOFlower / FedProxAES-256Python

The problem

Fixed-time traffic signals cannot react to changing congestion. A centralized vision system can react, but moving raw camera streams creates bandwidth and privacy costs.

The approach

Turn each intersection into an intelligent edge agent: detect local traffic, coordinate with neighbouring nodes, and exchange model weights instead of raw video.

System pipeline

01PerceptionYOLOv11n detects vehicles and BoT-SORT maintains tracks at the edge.
02Traffic stateDensity, maximum wait and emergency-priority signals become the local control context.
03CoordinationA graph-based look-ahead mechanism monitors downstream occupancy and suppresses spillback when pressure crosses the threshold.
04FederationFlower + FedProx aggregates local model updates while raw video remains on the edge nodes.
05SimulationEclipse SUMO + TraCI provides the digital test environment for isolated versus coordinated control.

My contribution

Evidence

The repository documents a YOLOv11n training run, a 12-class detection setup, a two-node SUMO validation environment, and a federated workflow targeting roughly 5–10 MB per node instead of raw video exchange.
