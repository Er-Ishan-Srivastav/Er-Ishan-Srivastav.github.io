03 / PAYFLOW · 2026 · C-DAC MINOR PROJECT

Database correctness as a product feature.

A simulated UPI management system built for the C-DAC PGCP-BDA minor project, using Flask + MySQL 8 to expose transactions, concurrency, database-side logic, fraud rules and auditability through working application flows.

Flask 3MySQL 8SQLAlchemy 2Stored ProceduresTriggerspytest

The problem

Financial workflows are not simple CRUD. A transfer has to preserve consistency when validation fails, concurrent requests arrive, or fraud and audit logic are triggered around the transaction.

The design

Keep important database semantics visible: ACID control, row-level locking, optimistic concurrency, constraints, views, stored procedures, triggers and indexed access paths.

Transfer lifecycle

01ValidateResolve the receiver, ownership and available balance before starting the transfer.
02LockThe transfer procedure uses database transaction control and row-level locking to protect sender and receiver state.
03TransferDebit, credit and transaction records are committed as one unit or rolled back together.
04AuditTransaction logs, status changes and database-side audit objects preserve traceability.
05MonitorFraud rules flag high-value, rapid-fire and repeated-failure patterns while reporting surfaces aggregate activity.

Dataset & verification

The seeded demonstration contains 1,000 users, 1,231 bank accounts, 1,000 UPI IDs, 2,970 beneficiaries and 10,000 transactions. The test suite covers authentication, transfers, rollbacks, fraud rules, complaints, history and concurrency scenarios.
